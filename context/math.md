# Mathematics generation context
Primary authority: sources/syllabus.pdf, “Syllabus 2a Mathematics Test”, pp. 1–3. Supporting material: sources/formulas.pdf, Mathematics v2019.1; sources/brochure.pdf, printed pp. 8–10. Local files were reviewed 2026-10-29. The supplied brochure specifies 60 minutes, no calculator, exact arithmetic, and a digital formula sheet.

Allowed topic labels and coverage:
- Functions and graphs (§1): compositions of polynomials, nth roots, powers, exponentials, logarithms, trigonometric and absolute-value functions; domains, ranges, limits, transformations, symmetry, asymptotes and inverses.
- Algebra and equations (§2): simplifying and rearranging expressions, substitution, equations and inequalities in standard functions, factoring, quadratic roots and simultaneous linear equations. Check domains and excluded denominator zeros.
- Differential calculus (§3): derivatives of standard functions, sum/product/quotient/chain rules, first and second derivatives, monotonicity, extrema, inflection points, slopes, tangents/normals, optimization, distance/velocity/acceleration.
- Integral calculus (§4): definite and indefinite integrals and integration constants; standard antiderivatives and linear inner substitutions cf(ax+b)+d; areas, volumes of revolution and mean values. Avoid advanced techniques not required by this scope.
- Trigonometry (§5): unit circle, radians/degrees, amplitude/phase/period/frequency, exact standard-angle values, symmetry/periodicity, equations and inequalities with linear arguments, Pythagorean/sum/difference/double-angle identities.
- Geometry (§6): areas/perimeters, volumes/surface areas, lines, circles, triangle properties, Pythagoras, sine/cosine laws, tangent/normal slopes and intersections.
- Vectors (§7): norm/direction, components, scalar multiplication, addition/subtraction, dot products, angles/orthogonality/distances, parametrized planar motion and speed/velocity/acceleration.

The actual supplied formula sheet has trigonometric identities and standard integrals, not a general collection of algebra or derivative rules. Use these formulas consistently and assume other techniques are known from memory. All question text and correct options must be self-contained. Do not require a diagram unless the mathematical data are also given in text.
